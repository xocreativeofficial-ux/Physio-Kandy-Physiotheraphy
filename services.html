WHO WE ARE

A modern approach to physical rehabilitation.

Physio Kandy is a physiotherapy and sport rehabilitation centre serving people looking for support with movement, recovery and physical performance.

Our approach is centred around understanding the individual and creating an appropriate rehabilitation pathway based on their needs.
